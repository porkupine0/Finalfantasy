# FF7 Remake Guide

A phone-friendly walkthrough and loot checklist for Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade (Normal difficulty, 100% first run), including the INTERmission Yuffie episode.

- **Chapters**: every chapter with its loot checklist, missable warnings, boss tips
- **Checklists**: filter all side quests, materia, weapons, manuscripts, music discs and Colosseum matches; search; hide what you have
- **Builds**: weapon / materia / accessory loadouts for Cloud, Tifa, Barret and Aerith by chapter range
- **Tips**: combat fundamentals, progression, a don't-miss list, minigames
- **Yuffie**: INTERmission chapters 1–2

Open `index.html`. Progress is saved on the device; works offline once loaded (service worker).
